# Discrete clusters vs gradational continuum (basin decorated assemblages)

Clustering-tendency tests on the first 4 CA axes of 28 basin assemblages.

- **Hopkins statistic = 0.71** (0.5 = no clustering tendency / continuum; >0.75 = clustered). 
- **Silhouette by k**: k=2:0.46, k=3:0.53, k=4:0.54, k=5:0.52, k=6:0.40; best k = 4 (max silhouette 0.54).
- **Gap statistic**: best k = 1 (Gap(k): +0.39, +0.48, +0.72, +0.93, +0.97, +0.93).

**Reading.** 
The gap statistic favors k = 1 and the best silhouette is 0.54; clustering tendency (Hopkins 0.71) and these indices are reported as found, and weak/modest cluster separation is consistent with a near-continuum rather than sharply bounded groups.
