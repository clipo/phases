# How many decorated sherds does an assemblage need?

Pairs of multinomial samples of size n from the basin's pooled profile (Gini-Simpson diversity 0.592), 4,000 draws per size. The two share one profile, so all of the F_ST between them is sampling.

Observed between-cluster F_ST at five clusters, 28 assemblages: **0.0307**.

| decorated sherds per assemblage | spurious F_ST, median | 95th percentile | median as a share of the observed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 0.0146 | 0.0630 | 48% |
| 50 | 0.0070 | 0.0305 | 23% |
| **75** | 0.0044 | 0.0195 | 14% |
| 100 | 0.0034 | 0.0156 | 11% |
| 150 | 0.0023 | 0.0105 | 7% |
| 200 | 0.0016 | 0.0074 | 5% |
| 300 | 0.0011 | 0.0052 | 4% |
| 500 | 0.0006 | 0.0030 | 2% |
| 1000 | 0.0003 | 0.0015 | 1% |

At 75 sherds the artifact's median is about a seventh of the observed differentiation and its 95th
percentile falls below it; above that the bias shrinks slowly. Mainfort (2003) kept sites with 800
or more sherds in all, and decorated sherds are 13 percent of his table, so his rule is about 100
decorated sherds. The analytic expectation for two equal samples from one profile is close to 1/n.
