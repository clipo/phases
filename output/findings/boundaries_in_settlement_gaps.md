# Do the phase boundaries run through the gaps in late-period settlement?

83 compiled sites with a period A or B component fall in the mapped area; 27 lie within 1 km of an analysed assemblage and are dropped, leaving 56 independent late-period sites.
Phase boundaries: 88 points at 1.5 km spacing. 300 alternative partitions of each kind, all carrying the phases' group sizes (8, 11, 9).

| kernel bandwidth | at the assemblages | along the phase boundaries | random-seed boundaries, median | share denser than the phases' | compact-partition boundaries, median | share denser than the phases' |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 km | 0.90 | **0.63** | 0.45 | 18% | 0.44 | 15% |
| 5 km | 1.01 | **0.82** | 0.77 | 40% | 0.73 | 14% |
| 8 km | 1.00 | **0.81** | 0.86 | 64% | 0.81 | 61% |

Densities are other late-period sites per 100 km^2. "Share denser" is the share of alternative
partitions whose boundaries run through MORE settlement than the phase boundaries do. A high share
means the phase boundaries sit in unusually empty country even for boundaries of their own kind;
a share near half means they are ordinary in that respect. The placement is descriptive (rule 18).
