# Time-aware emergence of phase-like structure (n = 28)

Drift field on the real layout at the positive-control corner, copying kernel on river-network (along-waterway) distance: interaction length 24 river-km, mixing 0.02, innovation 0.012, 1800 steps, 90 slices, window 8; 500 realizations. CA1 inertia (observed) = 0.48. Focal node: Parkin.

## Probability of sharing Parkin's emergent community
- Contemporaneous (pure spatial drift) snapshot, shown in Figure 8A: mean P = 0.36 (range 0.15-0.73) across the other 27 assemblages; co-membership falls with distance from Parkin (Spearman rho = -0.92 on river-network distance, -0.83 straight-line), the intuitive spatial gradient.
- Time-transgressive sampling: mean P = 0.68 (range 0.06-0.85); co-membership still falls with distance from Parkin (Spearman rho = -0.42), less steeply than in the contemporaneous snapshot (-0.92). Assemblages at different seriation positions carry different repertoires, so membership reflects space and time jointly.
- Either way, no assemblage is a certain member (P = 1) or non-member (P = 0) of Parkin's group: membership is graded and probabilistic, so the 'phase' has no stable membership.

The phase is the fuzzy, spatially structured residue of drift, not a society.
