# Time-aware emergence of phase-like structure (n = 43)

Drift field on the real layout at the positive-control corner, copying kernel on river-network (along-waterway) distance: interaction length 24 river-km, mixing 0.02, innovation 0.012, 1800 steps, 90 slices, window 8; 500 realizations. CA1 inertia (observed) = 0.48. Focal node: Parkin.

## Probability of sharing Parkin's emergent community
- Contemporaneous (pure spatial drift) snapshot, shown in Figure 8A: mean P = 0.33 (range 0.18-0.72) across the other 42 assemblages; co-membership falls with distance from Parkin (Spearman rho = -0.83 on river-network distance, -0.44 straight-line), the intuitive spatial gradient.
- Time-transgressive sampling: mean P = 0.71 (range 0.05-0.91); co-membership no longer falls with distance (Spearman rho = -0.16, against -0.83 for the contemporaneous snapshot). Assemblages at different seriation positions carry different repertoires, so membership reflects space and time jointly.
- Either way, no assemblage is a certain member (P = 1) or non-member (P = 0) of Parkin's group: membership is graded and probabilistic, so the 'phase' has no stable membership.

Interpretation: across many drift realizations on the real geography, with no boundary imposed, membership in Parkin's emergent community is graded and probabilistic rather than marking a bounded set, and the between-group cultural F_ST sits at the observed level rather than the much higher value a bounded group would leave. The phase is the fuzzy, spatially structured residue of drift, not a society.
